9 November 2026 · v1.0

Version 1.0

  • Lock screen and keyboard media keys control playback, and the screen stays awake while it plays.
  • Playback picks itself back up after a phone call or Siri.
  • A live line under Play says what's driving the sound right now.
  • Tighter timing: earthquake notes land on the beat in Pulse.
  • Loudness presets for WAV and video export: Socials, Streaming or Raw.
  • Share this moment: a story-sized image of what you just heard.
  • Delayed feeds show their last real reading and its age instead of a dash.
  • Live data refreshes about every 10 minutes for real, served from earthmidi.com.
  • Fonts and code now load from earthmidi.com only; the browser refuses connections to anything else.
  • Bigger tap targets, a full Reduce Motion mode and screen reader announcements for big events.
  • New pages: support, press kit and this one. Plus an embeddable player.

Simpler and calmer
