2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Earth MIDI turns live public science data into music. Earthquakes ring bells pitched by depth and struck by magnitude, the solar wind sweeps a synth pad, ocean swell and tides shape drones, and city temperatures tune a chord. Audience: musicians and producers who want a living, generative source of melody and MIDI; listeners who want ambient sound for focus or sleep; teachers and curious people who want to hear what the planet is doing right now. The problem it solves: raw earthquake and space-weather data is hard to feel; Earth MIDI makes it audible in real time and lets producers route it into their DAW as MIDI.

3. HOW TO USE (no setup or credentials needed)
- Launch. On the welcome card tap Ambient (or Pulse for beats). Sound starts.
- PLAY/STOP button starts and stops sound at any time.
- Bottom bar: Readings (native map plus live values, quake list with the note each one played), MIDI (virtual CoreMIDI port "Earth MIDI": open GarageBand or any MIDI app and choose it as input; Bluetooth and Network MIDI also supported), AirPlay, Settings.
- Lock the phone while playing: Lock Screen controls and a Live Activity / Dynamic Island appear.
- Widgets: long-press the Home Screen, add Earth MIDI widgets (space weather, biggest quake, play button).
- Siri / Shortcuts: "Play Earth MIDI", "What's the space weather on Earth MIDI".
- Record / Export: saves WAV or MIDI through the share sheet.
The sound engine is HTML/JavaScript bundled inside the app and run by WebKit; it does not load remote code.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES (read-only public data, no accounts, no personal data sent)
- earthmidi.com (our own site): a cached snapshot of the feeds below, refreshed every 10 minutes
- USGS Earthquake Hazards Program (earthquake.usgs.gov): earthquakes
- NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (services.swpc.noaa.gov): solar wind, Kp index, X-ray flares
- NOAA Tides and Currents (api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov): tides
- NASA EONET (eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov): wildfires and storms
- Open-Meteo (api / marine-api / air-quality-api.open-meteo.com): weather, ocean swell (Copernicus data), air quality
- Where The ISS At (api.wheretheiss.at) and CelesTrak (celestrak.org): space station position
- Apple MapKit: the Readings map
No analytics, no tracking, no AI services. No AI runs in the app: every note comes from a data reading through the same rule every time. No authentication or payment services.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. The data is global and identical for all users; there is no region-specific content or features. Only the device's language and time zone affect formatting.

6. DATA RIGHTS
