## Description
Earth MIDI turns the living planet into music, live.

Every earthquake rings a bell, pitched by its depth and struck as hard as its magnitude. The solar wind
sweeps a synth pad, ocean swell breathes at its real wave period, tides bend a drone, the jet stream howls,
and ten cities sing a chord tuned to their temperature right now. When the sun flares, bells ring.
The planet's magnetic weather even picks the musical scale.

• Live: hear events as the sensors publish them, or replay the last 24 hours in three minutes
• Ambient or Pulse: a drifting soundscape, or drums and bass driven by the solar wind
• Keeps playing in the background and on the Lock Screen, with a Live Activity and Dynamic Island
• Feel big quakes with haptics
• Widgets: space weather, the day's biggest quake and a play button on your Home Screen
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Play Earth MIDI", "What's the space weather on Earth MIDI"
• MIDI out: Earth MIDI appears as a MIDI source in GarageBand, Logic, Ableton, AUM and more, with clock and CC lanes
• Record and export WAV, stems and MIDI, free to use with credit
• AirPlay to any speaker
• On Mac: a menu bar player and a virtual MIDI port for your DAW
• On Apple TV: an ambient planet for your living room

Real data from USGS, NOAA, NASA, Open-Meteo and Copernicus. AI helped design and build Earth MIDI,
including the rules that turn data into sound. But no AI runs while you listen: every note comes from a
live sensor reading through the same rule every time.

## Review notes
Free app, no login. Tap PLAY (or pick Ambient on the welcome card) to start sound. Native features to try:
lock the phone while playing (Lock Screen controls + Live Activity), the Readings button (native map + live
values), the MIDI button (virtual CoreMIDI port: open GarageBand and pick "Earth MIDI" as a track input),
widgets, and Siri "Play Earth MIDI". The sound engine is HTML/JS run by WebKit inside the app and bundled
with it; live readings come from public scientific data feeds.
