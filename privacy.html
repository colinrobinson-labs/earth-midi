Privacy

Earth MIDI website and apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV · updated 7 November 2026

The short version

Earth MIDI has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The apps collect no personal data at all.

The apps

  • No sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your settings, presets and recordings stay on your device. Exports only leave it when you share or save them yourself.
  • The apps download public sensor data (earthquakes, space weather, ocean, weather, tides, natural events) through earthmidi.com and from the public sources listed on the site. Like any app that goes online, those servers see your IP address; we don't log or keep it.
  • Bluetooth and local network are used only if you choose to connect a MIDI device or turn on Network MIDI.

The website

Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Settings stay in your browser.
