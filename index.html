      Use it in your DAW

      • Live MIDI on Mac: Audio MIDI Setup → MIDI Studio → IAC Driver → “Device is online”. Pick it above, arm a MIDI track.
      • Windows: install loopMIDI, create a port, pick it above.
      • Sync: Producer controls → MIDI → “Send MIDI clock” and your DAW follows the Sun's tempo.
      • No live MIDI? Export the data pack and drag the .mid in. Each layer has its own channel and CC lanes.

      Accuracy

      Replay compresses the last 24 hours, keeping every event in its real order and spacing. The clock shows the real UTC time being played. Live mode plays new quakes within about a minute of USGS publishing them. Solar wind is measured at L1, 30–60 minutes before it reaches Earth. The ISS position is propagated from its published orbital elements and checked against a live fix (the gap is shown on its card). Sunrise times are computed. If any source fails, the status bar says demo for that feed and nothing is passed off as live.
